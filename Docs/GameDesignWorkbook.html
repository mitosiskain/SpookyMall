Narrative Architecture: The Missing Auditor & The Multi-Floor Pocket Mall

Layer 1: The Hook

The Elena Vance Contract

Hired to locate Elena Vance, an urban architectural auditor who vanished inside the condemned Arcadia Megamall. The entrance seals behind you at 03:17 AM. Her first major camp & trail is discovered on Floor 3.

Layer 2: The World

"Mall City" & Residual Ghosts

Survivors built sanctuaries around the Central Oval: Quartermaster Chen's Flash & Phantom Surplus, the North Night-Market Food Court, and the South Geothermal Bathhouse & Revival Sauna.

Layer 3: The Upper Climb

The Geodesic Ark Canopy

Why do hallways bend by 1.5°–4° per bay, and why is the Floor 6 roof framed with Vaulted Spaceship Hull Ribs & Biotic-Cyan Neon? Elena climbed toward the Roof Control Ring to override the containment lock.
